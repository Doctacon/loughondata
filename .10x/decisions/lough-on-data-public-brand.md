# Use Lough on Data as the public contractor brand

## Context

Connor Lough is operating through Quire LLC while establishing an independent data-engineering practice. The existing site is a personal technical blog and identifies Connor as employed by Harness. It does not communicate a contractor offer, target buyer, or contact path.

## Decision

The public-facing business identity MUST be **Lough on Data**. The site MUST present Connor as an independent data engineer and use the existing portrait as part of that personal-operator identity. Quire LLC MUST appear only in a footer/legal disclosure as the legal entity behind the public brand.

The practice targets growing data teams and early-stage startups worldwide through remote engagements. Its primary offer is fixed-scope data-system projects plus ongoing advisory; it is not positioned as general staff augmentation.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the site as a personal blog:** Rejected because it does not convert prospective clients or explain an active professional offer.
- **Lead publicly with Quire LLC:** Rejected because Lough on Data better matches the existing domain, public reputation, and technical writing.
- **Use a detached consultancy brand without Connor’s portrait:** Rejected because the practice is a single independent operator and personal credibility is an asset.
- **Publish pricing, fixed packages, or contractor rates:** Rejected for the first release. The site will use an email-first conversation about the prospect’s problem and context.
- **Name Harness as past experience:** Rejected for public copy. Prior in-house data-engineering experience may be described without naming the employer or disclosing confidential details.

## Consequences

- The public site needs a contractor-focused homepage, services page, selected-work page, and revised About page.
- Site language must be warm and personal while remaining technically credible.
- The existing writing archive remains a separately navigable journal, rather than the homepage’s primary conversion content.
- Contracts, invoices, and other legal/commercial materials may use Quire LLC independently of this public identity.
