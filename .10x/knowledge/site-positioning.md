# Site positioning

## Current business context

Connor Lough has left Harness and is operating as a contractor through Quire LLC. Public-facing business identity is **Lough on Data**.

## Ratified positioning for the website facelift

- Primary audience: growing data teams and early-stage startups that need reliable data foundations without immediately adding permanent headcount.
- Primary conversion: email contact, not scheduling-first. The approved CTA is “Tell me what’s brittle,” asking for the unreliable problem, who relies on it, and the needed outcome.
- Lead offer: an independent data engineer delivering focused projects and advisory engagements with a clear problem, scope, and next step—not open-ended staff augmentation.
- Public credibility uses Databox, Buoy Search, public open-source work, and non-confidential prior in-house experience. Do not name Harness or disclose confidential employer/client details.
- Services are Platform Assessment, Pipeline Buildout, Warehouse & Modeling, and Data Reliability; their technical stance is principles-first, portable, auditable, and open-source-friendly.
- Tone is warm and personal: practical technical partnership, grounded in useful problems rather than generic consultancy marketing.
- Public brand is Lough on Data; Quire LLC appears only as the footer/legal entity disclosure.

## Implications

The site should read less like a personal blog/portfolio and more like a consulting business home base backed by strong personal proof. Blog posts and projects remain useful evidence, but the homepage should quickly answer:

1. What problems Connor solves.
2. Who he solves them for.
3. Why he is credible.
4. What engagement shape is available.
5. How to contact him.

The public brand should be **Lough on Data** even though the legal entity is Quire LLC.
